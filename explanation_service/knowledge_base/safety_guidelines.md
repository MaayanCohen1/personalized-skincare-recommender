# Safety Guidelines

Patch testing is a widely recommended practice before introducing any new skincare product into a routine. Dermatological guidelines suggest applying a small amount of the product to a discreet area of skin, such as behind the ear or on the inner forearm, and waiting 24 to 48 hours to observe for any adverse reaction. This simple precaution helps individuals identify potential sensitivities before applying the product to larger areas.

Sunscreen use is considered one of the most important steps in any skincare routine. The Skin Cancer Foundation and the American Academy of Dermatology both recommend applying a broad-spectrum sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher daily, regardless of weather conditions. Reapplication every two hours, or immediately after swimming or perspiration, ensures continued protection from ultraviolet radiation exposure.

When combining active ingredients, it is important to consider potential interactions that could lead to irritation. For example, using retinol alongside high-concentration alpha-hydroxy acids (AHAs) in the same routine step may compromise the skin barrier. Dermatologists commonly advise introducing one active ingredient at a time and allowing the skin to acclimate over several weeks before adding another potent active to the regimen.
