# Routine Category Fit Guide

A cleanser for dry or sensitive skin is usually a better fit when it is described as gentle, non-stripping, soothing, or hydrating. Explanations for these users should emphasize cleansing without worsening dryness or discomfort.

A cleanser for oily or acne-prone skin is usually a better fit when it supports balanced cleansing, pore care, or light oil management without sounding harsh. Explanations should avoid implying that aggressive stripping is ideal.

A moisturizer for dry skin is usually a better fit when it supports hydration, moisture retention, softness, or the skin barrier. Explanations should connect the moisturizer’s role to comfort and reduced tightness.

A moisturizer for oily skin is usually a better fit when it is lightweight, non-heavy, fast-absorbing, or oil-free while still supporting hydration. Explanations should emphasize balanced moisture rather than rich nourishment.

An SPF for dry or sensitive skin is usually a better fit when it combines daily UV protection with hydration or skin comfort. Explanations should connect the product both to protection and to how it feels on dry or reactive skin.

An SPF for oily or combination skin is usually a better fit when it is lightweight, non-greasy, or shine-aware. Explanations should emphasize daily protection with a lighter finish rather than rich moisture.

A serum is usually an optional or focused step rather than the foundation of the whole routine. Explanations should present serum benefits as supportive and targeted, not as if the serum replaces cleanser, moisturizer, or sunscreen.

A toner is usually an optional step unless it adds clear soothing, hydrating, refreshing, or balancing value. Explanations for toners should remain modest and should not overstate their importance.

When a product is marked as essential in the routine, the explanation can frame it as a core daily-support step. When a product is optional, the explanation should frame it as an added-support step rather than a must-have.

If a product was selected with a high fit score for dryness, sensitivity, or oiliness, the explanation should connect its category role to that user need. This helps the output explain why the product belongs in the routine, not just what an ingredient does.

A cleanser explanation should sound different from a moisturizer explanation. A cleanser should focus on cleansing style and comfort, while a moisturizer should focus on hydration, softness, and barrier support.

An SPF explanation should sound different from both cleanser and moisturizer explanations. It should connect the user’s skin needs with daily protection and the product’s finish or comfort profile.
