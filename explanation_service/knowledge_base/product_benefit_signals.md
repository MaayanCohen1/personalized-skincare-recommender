# Product Benefit Signals

Barrier support usually refers to helping the skin maintain comfort and reduce moisture loss. In explanations, this is most relevant for dry, sensitive, irritated, or dehydrated-looking skin.

Lightweight hydration refers to moisture support without a heavy, rich, or greasy skin feel. In explanations, this is often most relevant for oily, combination, or acne-prone skin.

Soothing support refers to helping the skin feel calmer and more comfortable. In explanations, this is especially relevant for sensitive, red, irritated, or dryness-linked discomfort.

Oil control should be framed carefully. It is most relevant for oily or combination skin and should not be strongly emphasized for dry or sensitive skin.

Pore-focused support is most relevant for oily or acne-prone skin. In explanations, it should be described conservatively and should not imply harsh or stripping care.

Non-stripping cleansing is especially relevant for dry, sensitive, irritated, or barrier-compromised skin. It can also be relevant for oily skin when the goal is balanced cleansing without rebound dryness.

A rich or cushioning texture is often more relevant for dry skin than oily skin. A lightweight or non-heavy texture is often more relevant for oily or combination skin than very dry skin.

Fragrance-free fit can be a helpful explanation point when the user has sensitive, dry-sensitive, or irritation-prone skin signals and the product metadata supports that point.
