# Ingredients Dictionary

Niacinamide is a water-soluble form of vitamin B3 commonly used in skincare. It is often included in products designed to support the skin barrier, improve the appearance of uneven tone, and help balance excess surface oil.

Hyaluronic acid is a humectant used in skincare to help attract and retain water near the skin surface. It is commonly included in products designed to improve hydration and skin comfort.

Sodium hyaluronate is a salt form of hyaluronic acid commonly used to support hydration and improve skin comfort. It is often included in lightweight formulas intended to help the skin feel smoother and more hydrated.

Glycerin is a humectant that helps draw moisture into the outer layers of the skin. It is widely used in skincare to support hydration and help maintain moisture balance.

Panthenol is a form of provitamin B5 commonly used in skincare to support moisture retention and skin comfort. It is often included in products designed for soothing and barrier support.

Ceramide NP is a skin-identical lipid commonly used to support the skin barrier and help reduce moisture loss. It is often included in products designed for dry, sensitive, or barrier-compromised skin.

Salicylic acid is a beta hydroxy acid commonly used in skincare for pore-focused support. It is often included in products intended for oily or blemish-prone skin.

Centella asiatica extract is a botanical ingredient commonly used to support skin comfort and soothing care. It is often included in products intended for sensitive or easily irritated skin.

Squalane is an emollient commonly used to support softness and reduce moisture loss without a very heavy feel. It is often included in products designed to improve comfort and smoothness.

Allantoin is a soothing ingredient commonly used to support skin comfort and reduce the feeling of irritation. It is often included in gentle or barrier-supportive products.

Tocopherol is a form of vitamin E used in skincare for antioxidant support. It is often included in formulations that aim to support overall skin comfort and formulation stability.

Zinc oxide is a mineral UV filter commonly used in sunscreen formulations for broad-spectrum sun protection. It is often considered suitable for skin that needs a gentle-feeling sunscreen option.

Titanium dioxide is a mineral UV filter used in sunscreen formulations to support sun protection. It is often included in products intended for daily UV defense.

Fragrance is used to improve the scent of a product, but it may be a concern for some users with reactive or fragrance-sensitive skin.

Parfum is a fragrance label used in cosmetic formulations and may be a concern for fragrance-sensitive users.

Alcohol denat is a fast-evaporating alcohol used in some formulations for texture and finish. It may feel drying for some users, especially when skin is already dry or sensitive.


Retinol is a derivative of vitamin A commonly used in skincare to support the skin's natural cell turnover process. It is often included in products designed to improve the appearance of fine lines and uneven texture over time

Vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid) is a potent antioxidant commonly used in skincare to help neutralize free radicals generated by UV radiation and environmental pollutants. It is often included in products designed to support a brighter and more even complexion

Zinc oxide is an inorganic mineral compound commonly used in physical sunscreens to provide broad-spectrum protection by reflecting or scattering UV photons. It is often included in products recommended for sensitive skin

Aloe vera gel is a botanical extract containing polysaccharides and glycoproteins commonly used in skincare to provide moisturizing and soothing properties. It is often included in products applied to help calm irritated or sun-exposed skin

Alpha-hydroxy acids (AHAs), such as glycolic acid and lactic acid, are exfoliating acids commonly used in skincare to support skin renewal. They are often included in active regimens, though they should be introduced gradually to avoid compromising the skin barrier

Azelaic acid is an exfoliating acid commonly used in skincare for its multi-tasking properties. It is often included in products designed to target uneven tone and support blemish-prone skin

Tocopherol is a form of Vitamin E and a potent antioxidant commonly used in skincare to help protect the skin from environmental stressors. It is often included in formulations to support skin health and neutralize free radicals

Bisabolol is a botanical active commonly used in skincare for its calming properties. It is often included in products designed to support skin comfort and soothe easily irritated skin

Peptides are short chains of amino acids commonly used in skincare to support the skin's structural proteins, such as collagen and elastin. They are often included in products designed to support a firmer and more resilient appearance
